About me

I’m Matin Baderestani. Gridlab is my own Formula 1 project. I wanted to look at a race the way a team analyst does: who was quick in which sector, who won on strategy, and exactly where a pass happened.

I build and improve it in my own time and keep adding the things I want to see myself. If something looks wrong or you have an idea, I’d like to hear it.

About the site

Gridlab turns free public data into race analysis you can explore. From 2023 it uses the OpenF1 API for lap times, sectors, mini-sectors, tyre stints, pit stops, overtakes and car telemetry. For 2020 to 2022 it uses the Jolpica archive for results, qualifying, lap charts and pit stops.

It runs entirely in your browser, has no accounts and no ads, and is hosted free on GitHub Pages. It is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Formula 1 or the FIA.
